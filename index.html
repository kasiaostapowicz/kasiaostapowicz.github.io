About me

Connecting Earth Observation & Environmental Science

I am a research scientist specialising in Environmental Data Science and Earth Observation, with a focus on Land System Science, biodiversity and conservation, and climate change. My research addresses land-use and land-cover dynamics, ecosystem responses, and human–environment interactions across temperate, boreal, and Arctic regions.

I design and apply spatial modelling frameworks integrating multi-sensor satellite time series, machine learning, and GeoAI to quantify long-term environmental change and climate-driven ecosystem transformations.

Alongside research, I have led laboratories and international projects and supervised students at all degree levels.
